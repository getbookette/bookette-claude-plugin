# 7. Findings for the accountant

Write `out/findings.json`: open items such as method comparisons the script produced, unreadable documents, near-duplicates, missing receipts, personal-use questions, missing rules data, a prior-accumulation disagreement between a recorded carryforward and the schedule, a document that booked no line because its only payment lies outside the tax year, and a line, or a staged property-tax payment, whose entity cannot be established from the documents, the answers or the profile. Each finding: `kind`, `message`, `cites` (files or ledger ids — copy a ledger id from `out/ledger.json` after the assignment step, never a number you chose). A finding about a trip as a whole, such as a missing per-diem rate, cites `trip:<key>` — the trip's `key` in `out/profile.resolved.json`, never its name — with any of its ledger lines, never a document chosen to stand for the trip; `check_outputs.js` refuses a `trip:` cite naming no profile trip. `kind` is one of: `missing_receipt`, `related_party`, `ownership_split`, `fair_rental_days`, `near_duplicate`, `method_comparison`, `missing_rules_data`, `prior_accumulated_mismatch`, `unreadable`, `personal_use`, `outside_tax_year`, `entity_unestablished`, `note`, `trip_purpose_not_stated`; never coin a kind. Write a `note` only for a fact the accountant should see that no other kind names, such as a corrected form superseding its original: one sentence naming the documents, dates or vendors, with no amount and no treatment, and never a fact the person could answer — ask that in step 4. One exception is step 3's: a document whose placed income books to one place while other income under the same vendor waits for its place, whether or not a pass-through column is open, and an open pass-through column while the export's gross does not book to exactly one place `profile.json` stages. The other is step 4's Recommended placements paragraph: the note naming why a rent stream's placement recommends the one rental left. Neutral wording per `rules/policy.md`. A finding's message names the documents, lines, dates and vendors it is about and states no amount: the amounts are on the cited lines and in `out/books.json`, which the report shows from the build. Write "both trip meal readings are in books.json", never the readings; name a card row by its vendor and date, never its amount. Write counts without thousands separators. `check_outputs.js` refuses a finding message carrying a money figure.

- Write a `method_comparison` finding for the build's `method_comparison` rows: for the `home_office` row, one finding naming the home office and citing exactly the documents its home costs come from - each home cost's `source_file` as `out/home_costs.json` (or, while it is absent, `confirmed/home_costs.json`) spells it, except a cost dated outside the tax year, which feeds neither reading and is listed in `outside_year`, and nothing else (no entity key, ledger id or other document), so the finding keeps one identity from run to run - saying both readings (simplified and regular) are in books.json and the method is the accountant's choice - `check_outputs.js` requires one then; for `improvement_repair` rows, one finding citing their ledger ids, saying both readings (expensed as a repair, or capitalized and depreciated) are in books.json and naming any line whose class is not established; for `expense_macrs` rows, one finding naming each asset, saying both readings (expensed in year one, or depreciated under MACRS) are in books.json and naming any asset whose class is missing. Name no amount, no limit figure and no reading as the one that applies.
- Write a `fair_rental_days` finding when an entity's kind is `short_term_rental`: the message names the day counts the profile records — the rental days, the personal days and the stay count — says the predicates are on the report, and says whether a services note was recorded, and names no schedule.
- Write a `personal_use` finding when a personal-use day count is present: the message names the day counts and that the ratios are on the report.
- Neither finding is required: the step-8 check passes a run that writes neither.
- Write a `near_duplicate` finding naming both documents when a newly staged document restates a bill whose cost `confirmed/home_costs.json` already carries (a re-upload or a replacement): the confirmed cost stays, so stage no second one and the cost is never counted twice.
- Write a `missing_receipt` finding when a document shows a home office without stating its area ("the home office's area is not on record"), when the build prints a `missing_receipt` row because the `home_office` row carries no regular reading ("the regular home-office reading needs the home's area or costs") - `check_outputs.js` requires one then - and when a home cost sits inside a mixed export you did not stage.
- Write an `outside_tax_year` finding only for a document that booked no line because its only payment lies outside the tax year, such as a property-tax installment paid the next January (`reference/situations.md`); it cites that document, never a ledger id, and `check_outputs.js` refuses one citing a ledger id. A line, a staged property-tax payment or a home cost dated outside the tax year needs no finding: the build lists it in `books.json`'s `outside_year`, which the report and the export show to the accountant.
- Write an `entity_unestablished` finding when a staged property-tax payment's parcel no entity claims or its receiving entity is outside Schedules C and E - `check_outputs.js` requires one then; cite the payment and the bill.
- For each trip step 5 recorded with no purpose, and on a run staged without `confirmed/` each trip `profile.json` stages with no purpose too (that run's findings are the whole list the accountant reads), write one `trip_purpose_not_stated` finding citing `trip:<key>` and the trip's lines: the message names the trip's cities, its days and its place, and says the person has not said what it was for — "Chicago, IL, from 2026-08-17 to 2026-08-21, for Northpeak Consulting: the person has not said what the trip was for." — never a guess at one (`check_outputs.js` requires the finding while the purpose is `null`).
- For each recorded trip, write a `missing_receipt` finding citing `trip:<key>` for each thing the trip should carry and does not, one finding per gap, naming the day or days, with the gap's word in `gap`: a hotel for the nights away (a night inside a segment no stay covers; `lodging`); a ride to or from the airport on a day a ticket's leg flies (`ride`); a meal on each day away (`meal`); and, for a drive whose `miles` are not stated, one `mileage` finding: the days the mileage log leaves out ("The mileage log does not count the drive of 2026-07-10 to 2026-07-12"), or, for a trip whose `travel` is `own_car`, "The miles for the drive of 2026-07-10 to 2026-07-12 are not stated." in its place. A trip whose `miles` are stated takes no `mileage` finding, and no trip takes two. Write no finding for a gap the trip's `no_cost` lists: the person said the trip had no such cost. `gap` is one of `lodging`, `fare`, `ride`, `baggage`, `meal` and `mileage`, and only a trip's `missing_receipt` finding carries it (`check_outputs.js` requires it there, and refuses it elsewhere, where `no_cost` lists it, and on a second or a stated-miles `mileage` finding, `trip_finding_gap`). In a run without `confirmed/` that is every trip of `out/profile.resolved.json`, staged ones included; in an incremental run it is only the trips this run adds, since a trip `profile.json` stages had its gap findings written by the run that added it and an incremental run writes what is newly open (`reference/incremental.md`). A staged trip the person's `trip_register` answer makes a drive by own car with no miles stated, when it was not one as staged, takes its miles finding in the run that applies the answer (`check_outputs.js` requires the miles finding whenever the run writes the trip's gaps, `trip_miles_finding`). A folio of the trip that could not be read is its `unreadable` finding, and the stay it would have shown stays a gap. Travel rows that date no trip (step 3: a fare and rides with no city) take one `missing_receipt` finding citing their lines, saying the ticket would show where they went, unless the row's own document is the receipt, which is the support the finding asked for.
- When step 5 writes a travel home `profile.json` did not stage, or on a run staged without `confirmed/` carries one `profile.json` stages with `"home_origin": "documents"` (a home an earlier run read), write one `note` finding citing the documents that agree on it (at least two distinct ones, each reading printing the city), naming them and the city.
- A trip with no place (step 5) is an `entity_unestablished` finding naming its city and days and citing its lines, beside the question step 4 asks about them.
- A line step 4 asks nothing about because its document points to no staged place (the No pointer, no placement bullet) is an `entity_unestablished` finding citing its lines and naming the document, saying it names no property, so the person places it from the books.

## The run summary

Last, write `out/summary.json` — `{"summary": "<text>"}` — the run's closing summary, which the person reads first on Review: at most 600 characters, in the person's words, saying what booked cleanly (the places named as `profile.json` names them), what needs a look and why (the documents named as the inventory prints them), what the open questions are mostly about, and what is still missing. It carries no money figure, treatment word, policy word, identifier or link, and no number the books did not compute (a count of documents is fine). `check_outputs.js` refuses a run without one, so the build lists it as missing until it is written, and scans it like a finding's message.
